### 🛠️ Key Capabilities & Features

#### 💻 Multi-PC Local Network Sync
- Connect up to 10 computers in your garage to a single centralized database.
- Front desk, service advisors, and mechanics work simultaneously in real time.

#### 🔐 Role-Based Access Control
- Granular permissions for **Administrators**, **Foremen/Managers**, and **Mechanics/Technicians**.
- Keep sensitive financial data secure while giving technicians full access to job sheets.

#### 📋 Vehicle Intake to Work Order Workflow
- Capture mileage, fuel levels, existing exterior damage, and customer requests at reception.
- Generate and print blank or pre-filled intake sheets.
- Convert intake sheets to official **Work Orders** with a single click.

#### 🔍 Pre-MOT & Technical Checklists
- Complete vehicle inspection forms: brakes, fluid levels, suspension, battery health, and a **4-point tire tread depth tracker** (FL, FR, RL, RR).

#### 💬 1-Click WhatsApp & Viber Customer Alerts
- Send automated, pre-formatted instant messages to notify clients when their vehicle is ready for pickup.

#### 📦 Smart Parts & Labor Catalog
- Autocomplete search for predefined spare parts and labor rates to build invoices and job cards in seconds.

#### 📊 Live Analytics & Registration Expiry Alerts
- Proactively track customer vehicles with registrations expiring within 15 days.
- Real-time dashboard showing active jobs, revenue trends, and client history.

#### 🛡️ Robust & Native
- **Silent Background Backups:** Automatic local backups on startup to prevent data loss.
- **Fast Keyboard Navigation:** Optimized for speed with Enter/Esc sequencing and high-contrast dark/light themes.
- **Professional PDF/A4 Printing:** Clean layouts with zoom preview for service sheets and inspection certificates.
